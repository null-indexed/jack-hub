# Romeo and Juliet: familiarisation read

English Literature, AQA 8702 Paper 1 Section A. Year 9 Spring (first read), deep study Year 10 Summer.

## The point of this read

Get the story, the people and the shape of the play into your head now, so that in Year 10 you're studying the language, not working out what happens.

## Before you read

Find a version with modern notes alongside, or watch a full production first (the National Theatre and the Globe have filmed versions). Watching then reading is fine. It's a play.

## Who's who (fill in as you go)

- The Montagues: Romeo, Lord and Lady Montague, Benvolio
- The Capulets: Juliet, Lord and Lady Capulet, Tybalt, the Nurse
- Others: Mercutio, Friar Laurence, Paris, Prince Escalus

For each, write one line on who they are and one on what they want.

## Track these as you read

- Each act: write three sentences on what happens.
- Turning points: where could things have gone differently?
- Time: how many days does the whole play take? Why might Shakespeare rush it?

## Themes to notice (no need to analyse yet)

Love, hate and conflict, fate, family and duty, youth versus age, time.

## Questions to think about

1. Who is most to blame for the deaths? Pick one and argue it.
2. The Prologue tells you the ending. Why would Shakespeare do that?
3. How is Mercutio different from Romeo?
