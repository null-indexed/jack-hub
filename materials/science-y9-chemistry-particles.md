# Chemistry 1.1: The particle model

OCR Gateway Chemistry (J258), Year 9, Autumn.

## What you need to be able to do

- Describe the arrangement and movement of particles in solids, liquids and gases
- Explain changes of state using the particle model
- Say what the particle model gets wrong (its limitations)

## Changes of state

Melting, freezing, boiling, evaporating, condensing, subliming. For each one, say whether energy goes in or comes out, and what happens to the forces between particles.

Changes of state are physical changes. No new substance is made, so they can be reversed.

## Limitations of the model

The simple model shows particles as solid spheres with no forces drawn, and ignores the space between them in a realistic way. Try to list two problems before you check.

## Self-test questions

1. Why can a gas be compressed but a solid cannot?
2. During boiling the temperature stays the same even though heat is going in. Where is the energy going?
3. Is dissolving sugar in water a chemical or a physical change? Why?
4. Name one limitation of drawing particles as hard balls.
