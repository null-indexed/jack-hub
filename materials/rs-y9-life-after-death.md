# What happens when we die?

Religious Studies, AQA GCSE A (8062), Year 9, Autumn.

## Why this matters

Life after death comes up in both the Christianity and Buddhism beliefs sections of Paper 1. You need to explain what each religion believes, refer to sources of authority, and evaluate.

## Christianity: key ideas to research

- Resurrection, and why Jesus' resurrection matters to Christians
- Judgement
- Heaven and hell, and different Christian views on what they are (a place, a state, separation from God)
- Purgatory, and which Christians believe in it

## Buddhism: key ideas to research

- Rebirth (samsara) and how it differs from the idea of a soul moving on
- Karma and how actions shape rebirth
- Anatta (no fixed self)
- Nibbana (nirvana) as the end of rebirth

## Your task

Make a two column comparison: Christianity on the left, Buddhism on the right. Rows: what happens at death, what decides what happens next, is there a final goal, is there a permanent self.

## Practice questions (AQA style)

- 1 mark: Which of these is a Buddhist teaching about what happens after death?
- 4 marks: Explain two contrasting beliefs about life after death.
- 5 marks: Explain two Christian beliefs about life after death. Refer to scripture or sacred writings.
- 12 marks: "What you do in this life matters more than what happens after it." Evaluate this statement.

For the 12 mark answer you need arguments for, arguments against, religious teachings, and a justified conclusion.
