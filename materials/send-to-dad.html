Send it to Dad

How to get your Gemini notebook work to Dad. Updated 4 November 2026.

Why email? Google won't let your account share a notebook with Dad. Email always works, it's private, and Dad's Claude can read it and turn it into study sheets for the hub.

Never upload your work to this website. Anyone on the internet can see the hub, so your own work goes by email only.

The one rule: the subject line

Every email starts its subject with Study notes: and then the topic. For example:

  • Study notes: chemistry atoms
  • Study notes: DNA reds and ambers
  • Study notes: maths Pythagoras

That's how Dad finds them. Send it to Dad's normal email, the one already in your contacts.

Way 1: copy and paste (quickest)

  1. In your notebook, find the answer, quiz or note you want to send. Use the copy button under the reply, or open the note and select all of it.
  2. Open Gmail and start a new email to Dad.
  3. Subject: Study notes: plus the topic.
  4. Paste, add one line about what you want help with, and send.

Way 2: a whole note or study guide

  1. If the note has an export option, pick Export to Docs. It opens in your Google Docs.
  2. In Google Docs go to File, then Email, then Email this file. Send it to Dad as a PDF with the same subject line.
  3. If that's blocked, use File, Download, PDF, then attach the PDF to a normal email.

Way 3: your paper notebook

  1. Take a clear photo of the page, flat and in good light.
  2. Email it to Dad with the subject Study notes: plus the topic. One topic per email is easiest.

What's worth sending

  • Your red and amber list for a topic.
  • A quiz you did, with the ones you got wrong.
  • Anything Gemini explained that still doesn't make sense.
  • Pages from class you want turned into a study sheet.

Don't send passwords or login details, ever.

What happens next

Dad's Claude reads your email and can make a study sheet for the hub, add questions for your study guide, or explain the tricky bit a different way. You'll see the new sheet on the hub.

You don't have to write neatly or explain much. "This bit, I don't get it" is a perfectly good email.
