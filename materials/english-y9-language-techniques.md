# Language techniques bank

English Language, Year 9, Autumn. AQA 8700 Paper 1 and Paper 2.

## Why this matters

Paper 1 Question 2 and Paper 2 Question 3 ask how a writer uses language. Naming a technique gets you almost nothing on its own. The marks come from explaining the effect on the reader, using the right word for the job.

## The core set to know

| Technique | What it is | Ask yourself |
|---|---|---|
| Simile | Comparison using "like" or "as" | What two things are compared and what does that make me picture? |
| Metaphor | Saying one thing is another | What qualities carry over? |
| Personification | Giving human traits to something non-human | Does it make the thing feel threatening, friendly, alive? |
| Alliteration | Repeated sounds at the start of nearby words | Is the sound harsh, soft, fast, slow? |
| Sibilance | Repeated "s" sounds | Hissing, whispering, calm, sinister? |
| Onomatopoeia | Words that sound like what they describe | Which sense is the writer pulling in? |
| Pathetic fallacy | Weather or setting reflecting mood | What mood is being set up before anything happens? |
| Rhetorical question | A question not meant to be answered | What is the reader being pushed to think? |
| Triple (rule of three) | Three items grouped together | Does it build, list, or overwhelm? |
| Emotive language | Words chosen to stir feeling | Which feeling, and why does the writer want it? |
| Juxtaposition | Two contrasting ideas placed together | What does the contrast reveal? |
| Short sentence | Deliberately brief sentence | Shock, tension, emphasis, finality? |

Word classes count too. Examiners reward comments on a single well chosen verb, adjective or adverb as much as on a named device.

## How to write about it (the pattern)

1. Point: what the writer is doing.
2. Evidence: a short quotation, a few words, not a whole sentence.
3. Effect: what the reader thinks, feels or pictures.
4. Zoom: pick one word from the quotation and explain its connotations.

## Your task

Take any opening paragraph from a novel on your shelf. Find three techniques from the table. For each, write the four steps above. Then check: did you explain the effect, or just name the device?
