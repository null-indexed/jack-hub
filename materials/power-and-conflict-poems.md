# Power and Conflict poetry: getting to know the anthology

English Literature, AQA 8702 Paper 2 Section B. Deep study Year 10 Spring.

## How the exam works

You get one named poem printed in the exam and have to compare it with another poem of your choice from the anthology. The second poem is not printed. That's why knowing all 15 well matters.

## Your task, a few poems a week

For each poem, after reading it:

1. Fill in the row above.
2. Write down one line that stuck with you and why.
3. Note one other poem from the list it could be compared with, and what links them.

## Useful groupings to test yourself on

- Power of nature
- Power of humans and how it fades
- Reality of war and its aftermath
- Memory
- Identity

Try sorting all 15 into these groups yourself. Some fit more than one.
