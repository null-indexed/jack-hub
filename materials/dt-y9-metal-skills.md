# Metal skills

Design and Technology, Pearson Edexcel GCSE (1DT0), Year 9, Spring. This is your current topic.

## What you need to know

Workshop skills now, and the theory behind them for the written exam.

## Ferrous and non-ferrous

Ferrous metals contain iron and most will rust. Non-ferrous don't contain iron. Sort these yourself before checking: mild steel, aluminium, copper, cast iron, brass, stainless steel, tin.

Alloys are mixtures of a metal with other elements. Which of the list above are alloys?

## Properties vocabulary

Hardness, toughness, ductility, malleability, tensile strength, conductivity (heat and electrical), corrosion resistance. For each metal you use in the workshop, say which property makes it suitable.

## Processes to be able to describe

- Marking out: scriber, engineer's square, odd leg calipers, centre punch
- Cutting: hacksaw, junior hacksaw, tin snips
- Shaping: files (cross filing, draw filing), bending
- Joining: rivets, soldering, brazing, welding, and the difference between temporary and permanent joins
- Finishing: polishing, dip coating, powder coating, painting, and why finishes matter for corrosion

## Your task

Photograph each stage of your workshop piece. For each photo, write the tool used, the process name, and one safety point. This becomes evidence and revision at the same time.

## Self-test questions

1. Why is aluminium used for drinks cans instead of mild steel?
2. Describe how to mark a hole centre accurately on mild steel.
3. What is the difference between soldering and brazing?
4. Give two reasons for applying a finish to a steel product.
