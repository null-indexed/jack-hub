# Resources

Where to go, by subject. Use these to check your own work and practise, not to copy answers.

## Part 1: Sri Lakshmi's recommendations

| Resource | What it's for | Subjects | Link |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dr Frost Maths | Free maths practice with worked help and topic tests. Use alongside Sparx. | Maths | [drfrost.org](https://www.drfrost.org) |
| Seneca Learning | Short adaptive lessons and quizzes that match the exam board spec. Pick OCR Gateway for sciences. | Sciences, also RS, English, Business | [senecalearning.com](https://senecalearning.com) |
| CGP revision guides and workbooks | Physical books, to get off the screen. Buy the version for the right board and tier. | All subjects | [cgpbooks.co.uk](https://www.cgpbooks.co.uk) |
| Past papers and mark schemes | The real thing. Do a paper, then mark it yourself against the official mark scheme. | All exam subjects | See exam board links in Part 2 |
| Mark scheme walkthrough videos | Watch someone compare an answer with the mark scheme line by line, to learn what earns marks. Search YouTube for the subject, board and "mark scheme walkthrough". | All exam subjects | YouTube |

How to use them together: learn a topic (Seneca or CGP), practise (Dr Frost for maths, Seneca quizzes for the rest), then test with a past paper question and mark it, using a walkthrough video if you're unsure why you lost marks.

## Part 2: Other useful resources

### All subjects

- [BBC Bitesize GCSE](https://www.bbc.co.uk/bitesize/levels/z98jmp3): pick the right exam board for each subject
- [Physics and Maths Tutor](https://www.physicsandmathstutor.com): past papers sorted by topic, for maths and sciences especially
- Exam boards (specifications, past papers, mark schemes): [AQA](https://www.aqa.org.uk) for Maths, English and RS; [OCR](https://www.ocr.org.uk) for Sciences; [Pearson Edexcel](https://qualifications.pearson.com) for DT, Business and the BTEC in IT

### Maths (AQA 8300 Foundation)

- Sparx Maths (school homework, keep this going)
- [Corbettmaths](https://corbettmaths.com): 5-a-day, videos and practice questions for every topic
- [Maths Genie](https://www.mathsgenie.co.uk): GCSE topics graded by difficulty, and past paper walkthrough videos

### English (AQA 8700 and 8702)

- Mr Bruff on YouTube: set texts, poems and exam question guides
- Mr Salles Teaches English on YouTube: grade 9 style walkthroughs of exam answers
- No Fear Shakespeare (SparkNotes) for Romeo and Juliet in modern English alongside the original

### Sciences (OCR Gateway Triple)

- Free Science Lessons on YouTube: short videos for each topic and the required practicals
- [Cognito](https://cognitoedu.org): videos and questions organised by spec point

### Religious Studies (AQA 8062)

- [RE:Online](https://www.reonline.org.uk): background on beliefs and teachings
- BBC Bitesize AQA RS A

### Design and Technology (Edexcel 1DT0)

- Rubreka and LearnKit (Mr Loveland's dashboard, school login)
- [Technology Student](https://technologystudent.com): materials, tools and processes explained with diagrams

### IT (BTEC Tech Award DIT)

- Pearson BTEC DIT specification and sample assessments
- Google Sheets and Microsoft Excel help pages for each function

### Business (Edexcel 1BS0)

- Tutor2u Business: topic notes and videos for Edexcel GCSE Business
- BBC Bitesize Business (Edexcel)
