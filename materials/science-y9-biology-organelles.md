# Biology 1.1: Cell organelles

OCR Gateway Biology (J257), Year 9, Autumn.

## What you need to be able to do

- Name the parts of animal, plant and bacterial cells
- Say what each part does
- Explain the differences between eukaryotic and prokaryotic cells
- Use a light microscope and work out magnification

## Key words

Eukaryotic, prokaryotic, organelle, magnification, resolution.

## Magnification

Magnification = image size ÷ actual size. Practise converting between mm, µm and nm (1 mm = 1000 µm).

## Self-test questions

1. Why do plant cells have chloroplasts but root cells usually don't?
2. Which organelle releases energy, and through which process?
3. Give two differences between a bacterial cell and an animal cell.
4. An image of a cell is 30 mm wide. The real cell is 0.06 mm. What is the magnification?
5. Why can an electron microscope show ribosomes when a light microscope can't?
