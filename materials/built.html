Dino Color

Game · Added 25 October 2026

An endless runner. Your dino races through four landscapes, each with its own hazards: desert cacti, frost spikes and owls, tropical flytraps and toucans, and volcanic lava rocks. Collect bonuses for +100 points and try to beat your high score.

  • Jump: tap, Space or the Up arrow. Hold for a higher jump.
  • Duck: hold or swipe down. Use it mid-air for a fast drop.
